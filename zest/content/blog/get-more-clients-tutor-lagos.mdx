Lagos is full of parents looking for good tutors. The problem isn't demand — it's that most tutors make it hard for parents to book a session. If you're still figuring out how to [start a coaching business](/blog/start-coaching-business-nigeria), the same principle applies: make booking effortless.

If you're a tutor in Lagos and your scheduling process is "Send me a message on WhatsApp and I'll check my schedule," you're losing clients every day. The right [best scheduling software](/blog/best-scheduling-software-nigeria) eliminates this problem entirely.

Here's how to fix it.

## 1. Stop playing WhatsApp tag

The biggest waste of time for tutors is the back-and-forth:

- "Are you available on Thursday?"
- "Thursday morning works, what time?"
- "Can you do 4pm instead?"
- "Let me check..."

This conversation happens 20 times a week. That's 20 times you could have lost the parent to another tutor.

**Fix:** Create a booking link. Parents pick their own slot. Done.

With Zest, you can set up your availability once:
- Monday–Friday: 4pm–8pm
- Saturday: 10am–2pm

Parents see only the slots that work for you. They book, you get a WhatsApp notification. Zero back-and-forth.

## 2. Add intake questions

Before the first tutoring session, you need to know:
- What subject? (Maths, English, Science?)
- What level? (JSS1? WAEC prep? University?)
- What's the student's current challenge?

Instead of asking these on WhatsApp, add them as intake questions on your booking page. Every parent answers before they even contact you.

You walk into the first session already knowing what to prepare. That's professionalism.

## 3. Use your booking link everywhere

Put your Zest booking link in:

- Your WhatsApp status (pinned)
- Your Instagram bio
- Your Twitter/X bio
- Parent WhatsApp groups
- Your business card
- School notice boards

Every touchpoint should lead to one action: "Book a session here."

## 4. Offer a free discovery call

Some parents want to talk before committing. Create a separate event type:

- **Free 15-minute discovery call** — no intake questions, just a quick chat
- **Paid tutoring session** — full intake form, 60 minutes

This lowers the barrier. Parents who would never DM a stranger are happy to book a free 15-minute call.

## 5. Collect testimonials from parents

After 3–4 sessions, ask: "Would you mind sending a short message about your experience? I'd love to share it with other parents."

One parent saying "My son's Maths grade went from C to A" is worth more than any advert.

## 6. Set your rates clearly

Don't say "DM for pricing." Put it on your booking page:

- 1-on-1 tutoring: ₦15,000/session
- Group tutoring (3–5 students): ₦8,000/student
- WAEC prep package (8 sessions): ₦100,000

When parents see the price upfront, the ones who book are already comfortable with your rates. No awkward pricing conversations.
