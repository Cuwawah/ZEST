## 2. Calendly

**Best for:** Users who need integrations with Google/Outlook calendar

Calendly is the global standard, but it has limitations in Nigeria:

- Free plan: 1 event type, basic features
- Pro: $12/month (~₦18,000) — requires card
- No bank transfer option
- No WhatsApp integration
- No intake forms on free plan

**Downside for Nigerian users:** Requires a card for paid plans, and the pricing is in dollars.

Looking for more details? Here's our deep dive on [Calendly alternatives in Nigeria](/blog/calendly-alternative-nigeria).

## 3. Cal.com

**Best for:** Tech-savvy users who want open-source flexibility

Cal.com is an open-source Calendly alternative:

- Free self-hosted option
- Cloud plans from $12/month
- Good API and integrations
- No Nigerian payment support

**Downside:** More complex to set up, no local payment options.

## 4. Square Appointments

**Best for:** Businesses that also need point-of-sale

Square offers free scheduling with payment processing:

- Free for single users
- Built-in payment processing
- Not available in Nigeria

**Downside:** Not accessible from Nigeria.

## 5. Google Calendar Appointment Schedules

**Best for:** People already deep in the Google ecosystem

Google Calendar now has a built-in booking feature:

- Free with Google Workspace
- Simple to set up
- No custom intake forms
- No WhatsApp integration
- No bank transfer payments

**Downside:** Very basic, no intake forms, no local payment support.
