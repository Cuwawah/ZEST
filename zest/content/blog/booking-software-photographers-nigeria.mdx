Photographers in Nigeria face a unique challenge: every client interaction starts with a WhatsApp conversation that goes on for days.

"How much for a photoshoot?"
"Can you do next Saturday?"
"What packages do you have?"
"Let me confirm with my wife..."

By the time you've exchanged 30 messages, the client has booked someone else. It's the same problem [tutors face trying to get more clients](/blog/get-more-clients-tutor-lagos) — slow responses cost you money.

Here's how to fix your booking process.

## The problem with DM-based booking

If you're handling bookings through WhatsApp DMs:

- **You lose clients to slower responses** — someone else replies faster
- **You waste hours on questions** you could answer once on a booking page
- **You can't track who's booked** — everything is buried in chat history
- **You look unprofessional** — compared to photographers with proper booking pages

## What a professional booking flow looks like

Imagine this instead:

1. Client finds your Instagram
2. Clicks your booking link
3. Sees your packages and prices
4. Picks a date and time
5. Answers: "What type of shoot? (Wedding, portrait, product, event)"
6. Confirms booking
7. Gets a WhatsApp message with the details

That's it. No back-and-forth. No "let me check." Just a confirmed booking.
