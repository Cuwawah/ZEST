Starting a coaching business in Nigeria is one of the fastest ways to turn your expertise into a sustainable income. Whether you're a career coach, life coach, business coach, or fitness coach, the demand is growing — and the barriers to entry are lower than you think.

Here's how to go from idea to your first paying client.

## 1. Pick your niche

"Don't be a coach for everyone" is the most important advice you'll hear. The coaches who charge premium rates are specialists:

- **Career coaches** — help professionals navigate job transitions
- **Business coaches** — work with SME owners on growth strategy
- **Fitness coaches** — personal training and wellness programs
- **Health coaches** — nutrition, weight management, chronic conditions
- **Executive coaches** — leadership development for managers

Pick one. You can always expand later.

## 2. Register your business

You don't need a huge setup to look professional:

- **CAC registration** — Register as a business name (₦10,000–₦15,000) or limited company (₦50,000+)
- **Business bank account** — Kuda, Moniepoint, or Sterling all offer free business accounts
- **Tax ID (TIN)** — Free from FIRS, needed for invoicing

## 3. Define your offer

Before you sell anything, answer these:

- What specific problem do you solve?
- What does a session look like? (60 min? 30 min? 4 sessions?)
- What's your price? (Start at ₦15,000–₦50,000 per session for Nigerian markets)

Package your offer clearly. "1-on-1 career coaching — 4 sessions, WhatsApp support included" beats "I do coaching" every time.

## 5. Get your first clients

You don't need a website or social media following to start. Here's what actually works:

- **WhatsApp status** — Post your booking link daily. "Slots open this week for career coaching sessions. Book here →"
- **LinkedIn** — Share insights from your coaching. End posts with your booking link.
- **Referrals** — Offer your first 3–5 sessions at a discount in exchange for testimonials
- **Twitter/X** — Share quick tips. Build an audience of people who need your help.

## 6. Collect testimonials early

After every session, ask: "Would you mind if I shared a short testimonial from our work together?"

Social proof is everything. One good testimonial on your booking page can double your conversion rate.
